# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Rowberry is a place to build useful software together. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment, regardless of experience, background, age, disability, gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

This code applies to contributors, users, and maintainers in project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents Rowberry publicly.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and code, not someone's character.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions clearly, especially to new contributors.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Be considerate of people's time and differing experience levels.

Disagreement and rigorous technical review are welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, and degrading language are not acceptable. Neither are publishing another person's private information without permission, sustained disruption, repeated unwanted contact, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern or helps with a report.

## Report a concern

Email **[adam@spicey.dev](mailto:adam@spicey.dev)** to report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer. You may report something you experienced or witnessed. Do not open a public issue containing private details.

Include whatever you feel comfortable sharing: what happened, where and when, relevant links or screenshots, and any immediate concerns or preferred way to be contacted. A complete evidence package is not required to raise a concern.

Reports will be reviewed with care. Details will be shared only with people who need them to assess or address the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. The maintainer may ask follow-up questions and will communicate the outcome to the reporter where appropriate, while respecting others' privacy. There is no guaranteed response time.

If the report concerns the person receiving it, you may use the hosting platform's abuse-reporting tools instead. Maintainers involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome; where another uninvolved maintainer is available, they should handle it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit harmful content, request a change in behaviour, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces. The response should reflect the severity, impact, and repetition of the behaviour. Serious threats or harassment can lead to immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Anyone affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the same private email address and provide relevant context. An uninvolved maintainer should review the request where available. Attempts to evade restrictions or retaliate may result in further action.

Thank you for helping make Rowberry welcoming and constructive.
